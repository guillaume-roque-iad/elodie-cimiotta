Frequently asked questions

Your first points of reference.

Do I need previous property experience?

No. Both career changers and experienced professionals can get started. The ability to learn, organise your work and build trusting relationships is essential.

Is this a salaried job?

No. It is a self-employed activity. Before any decision, Élodie explains the status, costs, responsibilities and contractual terms applicable in the country concerned.

Can I start gradually?

That depends on your situation, availability and the applicable regulatory framework. The initial conversation helps build a realistic scenario and identify what needs checking.

How does the support work?

It combines iad training, conversations with Élodie, team meetings and follow-up on your action plan. The pace adapts to your experience and goals.

Can I develop my business in France or Spain?

Élodie has experience in both countries. She will guide you according to your business location, administrative situation and local network entry requirements.
