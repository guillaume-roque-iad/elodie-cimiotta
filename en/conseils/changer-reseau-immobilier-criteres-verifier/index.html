Changing property networks: what should you check before deciding?

Published 29 September 2026
Team Lucky editorial team · article prepared with AI assistance

Before changing property networks, do not compare only the advertised commission rate. Start with what is currently holding back your activity, then check five areas using concrete evidence: support, training, tools, the contract and the opportunity to develop a team. A useful decision rests on observable evidence and your own circumstances, never on an income promise.

1. Start by diagnosing your current network

A move may solve a lasting mismatch, but not necessarily a temporary difficulty. Write down three precise issues: a lack of practical answers, tools that duplicate work, training that is too general, costs that are hard to anticipate or insufficient support for accompanying a team.

Turn each issue into a testable criterion. Instead of “I want more support”, ask who answers an urgent question, through which channel, within what framework and who takes over during an absence. Also define three non-negotiable conditions. This helps you avoid deciding in the heat of frustration.

2. Observe real support, not just its presentation

Ask to attend a meeting, see how a professional situation is reviewed and speak to two different profiles: someone who has recently joined and someone already working independently. Look at the frequency of discussions, the mentor’s availability, the quality of feedback and how disagreements are handled.

iad’s page about choosing a network lists the quality of tools, organisation and the network’s strength among the points to examine. Professional source: iad France. This is a starting point; your checks should focus on what you will actually use in your area and daily work.

3. Test the tools through a complete workflow

A useful demonstration follows one case from end to end: create a contact, prepare an action, store the information, schedule a follow-up and retrieve the history. Note duplicate entry, access limits, available help and whether you can export your data within the applicable framework.

iad’s article on tools for independent professionals presents organisation, follow-up and visibility as uses to structure. Professional source: iad France, published 24 June 2026. Do not settle for a feature list: request a demonstration that matches the way you work.

4. Read the contract and plan the transition before announcing it

In France, a commercial agent’s remuneration, contract termination and entitlement to commission depend on the contract and circumstances. The official Service Public Entreprendre page on independent commercial agents explains the general framework. Check notice, current cases, costs, tool access, data and obligations that continue after termination. Seek appropriate legal or accounting advice if anything is unclear.

For another country, consult local rules: the translated versions of this article do not carry French law across borders. Do not announce a departure or move any data until your obligations are clear.

5. Check the conditions for developing and supporting a team

If this matters to your plans, ask how the mentoring role is learned, what resources exist for new advisers, how difficult situations are handled and what happens when you are unavailable. Also clarify recognition and remuneration rules in the contractual documents, without treating them as a guarantee of results.

A team environment is judged over time: quality of onboarding, mutual help, sharing of working practices and willingness to say when an answer needs checking. Look for a framework that lets you support others without sacrificing your own activity.

An evidence-based decision grid

For each criterion — support, training, tools, costs and contract, team development — classify the answer as confirmed by evidence, still to confirm or incompatible with my plans. Attach one piece of evidence: a contract extract, demonstration, timetable, named contact or discussion with an adviser.

Six questions: Who supports me after onboarding? Can I see the tool used on a real case? Which costs are fixed or variable? What does the contract say about leaving? How is a team supported? Who can help me compare the presentation with everyday reality?

Frequently asked questions

Should you choose the network advertising the highest commission?

No. Compare what you actually retain after applicable costs, as well as time saved, support, tools and the coherence of the contract. None of these elements guarantees an income.

Can you decide after one presentation?

A presentation helps you understand the model, but cannot verify everything. Ask for the relevant documents, a demonstration and conversations with several advisers.
